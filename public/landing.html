The problem

Design, manufacturing and the customer are disconnected

On one side, mass produced furniture. On the other, designers with good ideas and local workshops with real production capacity, with nothing connecting them.

The solution

We ship design, not furniture

ta connects those pieces with technology: you customize the design from home, and it gets built at a workshop near you. Less shipping, more suited to what you actually need.

One system, many forms

Modulo-a adapts to your space
